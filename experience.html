Aug 2025 – Dec 2025 · 5 months · Richmond, VA

Business Development Analyst — Lawn Die

4-person student consulting team, EastPoint Sports

Lawn Die, a $250 premium outdoor game from EastPoint Sports, needed a go-to-market strategy to stand out in a crowded market against established competitors like Spikeball and Cornhole. As part of a 4-person consulting team, my task was to lead the market research that would justify a specific, evidence-backed marketing strategy. I segmented 8 target customer groups using Claritas PRIZM data, benchmarked competitors' social media performance, and pulled consumer research from Mintel, YouGov, eMarketer, and Statista to build the case for a micro-influencer community strategy, complete with a specific budget allocation and ROI framework. The client was impressed by both the research and the final deliverable, and the team's combined recommendations projected $101K+ in revenue and $49K+ in profit.

May 2024 – Aug 2024 · Cape Town, South Africa

Research Lead — OceanEdge Consulting (diiVe)

One of two Research Leads, 8-person team, client: V&A Waterfront

The V&A Waterfront needed a scalable sustainability framework for its marine and industrial tenants but had no existing model to build from. As Research Lead on a team working directly with V&A's C-suite, my task was to build the research foundation the team's recommendations would rest on. I benchmarked global blue-economy programs, conducted 24 stakeholder interviews, and distilled 60+ pages of notes into a 15-page executive summary and 30-page discovery framework, while also helping shape the Solutions & Next Steps rollout plan. The result was a pilot-ready Blue Lease framework for 230+ tenants, with 80% of interviewed stakeholders supporting the concept ahead of its Q1 2025 rollout.
