Commercial Awareness

How I'm reading the market I'm entering.

Four trends I didn't just read about. Each one shows up directly in something I built.

Real Estate & Sustainability

Sustainability Is Becoming Part of the Contract

Green lease clauses are now standard practice rather than a rare add on. A 2025 industry survey found that 62% of new commercial leases include sustainability provisions, up from about half just a couple years earlier. This is the exact market I was working in as Research Lead on the Blue Lease project for the V&A Waterfront. The framework my team built for 230+ marine and industrial tenants is a direct, real world version of this national trend, not a case study I read about after the fact.

So what → It changed how I think about consulting work. A good recommendation can't just sound smart. It has to hold up legally and operationally, the same way our Blue Lease framework had to.
Marketing & Consumer Trends

Small Creators Are Outperforming Big Ad Spend

Brands are shifting marketing budgets toward micro influencers, creators with ten thousand to a hundred thousand followers, who get five to eight percent engagement according to eMarketer and Influencer Marketing Hub, compared to two to five percent for bigger creators. This is the research I personally led for the Lawn Die project. I used this exact data to build the case for our micro-influencer recommendation and to size the actual budget split behind it, rather than guessing at what would work.

So what → It changed how I evaluate marketing spend on any team I'm on. Reach matters less than trust and how well the audience fits, which is the whole argument our Lawn Die recommendation was built around.
Applied AI

AI Is Moving From One Model to a Team of Models

Anthropic's own 2026 research found that 57% of organizations already use AI agents for multi step workflows, and Gartner expects 40% of enterprise software to include task specific agents by the end of this year. My Generative AI course project put me directly inside that shift. I designed and built a six agent job search system, where each agent (intake, search, ranking, resume critique) handled one job instead of asking a single model to do everything, which is the same architecture the industry is moving toward.

So what → This matters for the data roles I want. Knowing how to design a system of agents, not just prompt one model, is becoming the real skill, and it's exactly what I practiced building that project.
Operations & Data Governance

Compliance Is Moving From Periodic Checks to Constant Monitoring

A 2025 compliance benchmark report found that 92% of organizations now run at least two audits a year, and 58% ran four or more, a clear jump from the old model of checking in once and hoping nothing slipped through. My SQL project for Parking and Transportation put this into practice at a smaller scale. I replaced the department's manual citation review with automatic triggers and logging that caught a 2.6% error rate the old process had been missing entirely.

So what → It's part of why I'm exploring supply chain and operations roles as part of my Hybrid Track. That project showed me firsthand how much of the current demand in that field is really about building this kind of automated check into a process, not just reporting on it after the fact.
