Commercial Awareness

How I see the market I'm stepping into.

Four trends I've seen firsthand. Each one shows up in something I built.

Real Estate & Sustainability

Sustainability Is Becoming Part of the Contract

Green lease terms aren't rare anymore, they're expected. A 2025 industry survey found that 62% of new commercial leases now include sustainability provisions, up from about half just a couple years ago. This is exactly the market I worked in as Research Lead on the Blue Lease project for the V&A Waterfront. The framework my team built for over 230 marine and industrial tenants was a real version of this trend, not something I studied after the fact.

So what → It changed how I think about consulting work. A good recommendation can't just sound smart. It has to hold up legally and operationally, the same way our Blue Lease framework had to.
Marketing & Consumer Trends

Small Creators Are Outperforming Big Ad Spend

Brands are shifting money away from big name influencers and toward smaller creators. Micro influencers, those with ten thousand to a hundred thousand followers, get five to eight percent engagement according to eMarketer and Influencer Marketing Hub, compared to two to five percent for larger creators. This is the exact research I led for the Lawn Die project. I used this data to build our case for a micro influencer strategy and to size the budget behind it, instead of guessing what would work.

So what → It changed how I evaluate marketing spend on any team I'm on. Reach matters less than trust and how well the audience fits, which is the whole argument our Lawn Die recommendation was built around.
Applied AI

AI Is Moving From One Model to a Team of Models

AI is moving from a single model doing everything to teams of models working together. Anthropic's 2026 research found that 57% of organizations already use AI agents for multi step workflows, and Gartner expects 40% of enterprise software to include task specific agents by the end of this year. My Generative AI course project put me right in the middle of that shift. I built a system of six agents, one for intake, search, ranking, and resume review, instead of asking one model to handle it all. That's the same direction the industry is heading.

So what → This matters for the data roles I want. Knowing how to design a system of agents, not just prompt one model, is becoming the real skill, and it's exactly what I practiced building that project.
Operations & Data Governance

Compliance Is Moving From Periodic Checks to Constant Monitoring

Compliance is shifting from occasional checks to constant monitoring. A 2025 compliance benchmark report found that 92% of organizations now run at least two audits a year, and 58% run four or more. That's a big shift from the old approach of checking once and hoping nothing slipped through. My SQL project for Parking and Transportation applied this idea on a smaller scale. I replaced manual citation reviews with automatic triggers and logging that caught a 2.6% error rate the old process had missed completely.

So what → It's part of why I'm exploring supply chain and operations roles as part of my Hybrid Track. That project showed me firsthand how much of the current demand in that field is really about building this kind of automated check into a process, not just reporting on it after the fact.
