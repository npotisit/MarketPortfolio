Data & Process · SQL · UR Business Process Optimization Project

Parking Citation SQL Optimization

3-person team, working directly with Parking & Transportation leadership

Parking & Transportation's citation workflow ran on fully manual review, with no way to systematically catch officer errors or track processing bottlenecks. Working directly with the department's Director and Assistant Director, my task was to redesign the data structure and automate the review process. I led the SQL development — building new tables, INSERT/UPDATE triggers, a stored procedure for officer-level mistake rates, and Tableau visualizations. The project uncovered a 2.6% manual citation error rate and cut query time from 0.1 seconds to under 0.0043 seconds.

Applied AI · Data Analytics · Generative AI Course

AI Job Search & Resume Critique Agent System

Led design and development

Job seekers without direct work experience often struggle to search multiple job boards efficiently and tailor their resume to each posting. For my Generative AI course, my task was to lead the design and build of an agentic system to automate this process end-to-end. I built a 6-agent workflow using Google's ADK — an intake agent, three parallel job-board search agents, a ranking agent, and a resume critique agent, all orchestrated by a coordinator with a self-correcting search loop. Testing it against my own profile, the system correctly followed instructions and returned 5–10 relevant listings across all three platforms.
